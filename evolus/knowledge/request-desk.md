# The described-lesson request desk

A support teacher, a class teacher or a parent asks for a lesson video to be described for a blind or low-vision child. The desk turns the request into a described lesson and sends it back.

## What a request needs

- **The video:** an https link to the video file, or the file attached.
- **The language** the child listens in: English or Italian.
- **Who it is for:** the child's age or class, and anything the teacher wants described with care (a formula, a map, an experiment).
- **When it is needed:** the date of the lesson.
- **Who to send it to:** the teacher's email.

If something is missing, the desk asks for it in one short reply rather than guessing.

## What the desk does

1. Reads the request and pulls out the link, language, age, date and email.
2. Starts the described lesson on Sottovoce and follows it until it is done.
3. Sends the teacher the link to the described lesson, the downloads (MP4, narration, WebVTT), and the lines that need a teacher's review, if any.
4. If the video cannot be read (a private link, too long, not a video), the desk says why and what to send instead.

## What the desk never does

- It never sends a line to a child that the checker did not verify or a teacher did not approve.
- It does not keep the child's name; the request only needs the age or class.
- It does not decide a child's support plan; that is the school's and the family's.
